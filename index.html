How a stacker works

Lift. Park.
Repeat.

Each platform lifts up to 2,000 kg at 2 m/min, so one footprint holds three cars.

  1. Drive onPark on the platform at ground level.
  2. LiftTurn the key or press the button. The platform rises at 2 m/min.
  3. Park belowThe next car parks on the ground underneath.
Level 2Level 1Ground

Car one rises to level 2.Car two rises to level 1.Car three parks below.
