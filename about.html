Pumice Auto Parking Systems is part of Pumice Infra Group, a manufacturer of customised car parking systems and pre-engineered buildings.

About Pumice

One team, from concept to car park.

Our team of professionals specialises in customised car parking systems and pre-engineered buildings, combining technical know-how with practical site experience.

We design, manufacture and install modern car parking systems and pre-engineered structures for industrial sheds, warehouses and infrastructure. Our aim is to be recognised as the leader in our industry, by consistently delivering high-quality products and service, personal attention and a commitment to excellence.

Every system is customised. We guide clients from the earliest concept stage, helping them plan and make the most of the parking space they have. Our range covers pit, ground-level, vertical and horizontal systems, so there is a solution for almost any site.
