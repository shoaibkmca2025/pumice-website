Seven stackers.
Every kind of site.

Two- and three-level systems, with or without a pit, run by a hydraulic power pack and a simple key or push button.

Ground + 1 · 2 cars · Dependent

Two Level Hydraulic Stacker

Two cars in the space of one. One parks on the ground, the other on a raised platform above it.

To bring the upper car down, the car on the ground is driven out first.

Greener by design. Hydraulic operation uses less electricity than motor-driven systems, the compact footprint needs less land, and the durable build needs less upkeep.

Two Level G+1 With Three Pole

A ground-plus-one hydraulic stacker on three poles, for sites that are tight on width but still need to take larger pallets.

Three Level G+2 Hydraulic System

Three cars on one footprint: one on the ground and two on platforms above.

To lower level one, the ground car is moved first. To lower level two, the ground and level-one cars are moved. Made for commercial and residential buildings where parking is limited, with minimal maintenance.

Two Level G–1 Ground

One car parks in a pit, the other on the ground pallet above it.

The ground car is moved first to reach the pit car. A hydraulic cylinder does the lifting, which keeps costs down. Ideal where headroom is restricted or maintenance access is difficult.

Three Level Pit + G + 1

Three cars across three levels: one in the pit, one at ground level and one above.

The ground-level car is moved to reach the upper or pit car. Pit depth depends on the clearance available above ground. Suited to commercial and residential buildings, with minimal maintenance.

Automated · Independent access

Hydraulic Puzzle Parking

Fully automated parking where any car can be retrieved without moving the others. Every platform works independently.

Spreads up to 10 columns wide and G+1 high, plus a pit level. Each level keeps one slot empty so platforms can slide and lift, and a PLC runs every move for smooth, error-free operation.

Mechanical Puzzle

The most cars on the smallest footprint. Retrieve any car without moving the rest.

Pallets move horizontally and vertically on preset programs. Call a car by entering its pallet number on the touch-screen panel. An entry-restriction sensor guards the bay, and on-screen error messages make troubleshooting quick.

Automated · Robotic

Tower Parking

Automated tower parking stores cars on vertical racks inside a multi-level structure.

Drive onto the ground-level platform and step away. Sensors, cameras and computer-controlled lifts move the car to a free slot. It needs far less ground than a conventional car park, ideal where land is scarce, and nobody spends time hunting for a space.

Automated · Up to 100 cars per module

Mini Rotary

Fully automated, with every platform independent. Any car comes out without moving the others.

Top and pit pallets move vertically while ground pallets slide horizontally, and cars on ground pallets simply drive off. Available motor-driven or hydraulic, for new construction or retrofit.
